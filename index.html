Merry Christmas, Brooke!

Zap the last-minute crowds with presents, finish the shopping list from Santana Row to Boston, and somehow get Adele a ticket.

Play full screen: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open the game from its new icon.

How to play

Shop fast. Zap kindly.

  • 👆Click or tap anywhere to walk there (the touchpad works). Hold and drag to steer.
  • 🎁Click or tap a shopper to throw them a present. They cheer up and wander off.
  • ⇦Prefer keys? Arrows or WASD to move, Space to throw. On a phone, the pink pad and ZAP button work too.
  • 🔋Each zap uses phone battery. It recharges by itself, and green chargers fill it fast.
  • 👇Follow the pink arrow. It always points to your next goal, and the Next box at the top says what to do.
  • ✨Shops with a sparkle over the door have something on your list. Walk into the door.
  • 💗Fill the Christmas Cheer meter and beat the boss to open the exit.
  • ☕Coffee is a speed boost. When the watch buzzes, Adele is texting or calling. Answer, then pick a reply.

Best on a laptop. A phone works too, in either direction.
